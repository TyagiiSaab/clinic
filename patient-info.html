Your first visit

Four simple steps.

1

Contact the clinic

Call, WhatsApp or send an appointment enquiry with your preferred day and time.

2

Confirm your slot

Wait for the clinic's confirmation by phone — an enquiry alone is not a confirmed appointment.

3

Arrive on time

Reach about 10 minutes early at the agreed time with your documents.

4

Bring your records

Carry relevant prescriptions, reports and ID as listed below.

What to bring ✓

  • Previous medical records relevant to your concern
  • Current prescriptions, if any
  • Recent test reports
  • A photo ID, if the clinic asks for one
Important information.
This website shares general clinic information and takes appointment enquiries — it is not a substitute for professional medical advice. This clinic does not provide emergency services. In a medical emergency, call local emergency services (e.g. 108 in India) or visit the nearest emergency department immediately.
